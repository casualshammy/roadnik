### Short description
Location sharing app without registration and ads.

### Full description
Introducing Roadnik - the perfect app for anyone who needs to share their location with loved ones! With Roadnik, you can easily share a link that allows your friends and family to track your movements on a map in real-time. Not only that, but they'll also be able to see your phone's battery level and cell signal strength.

What sets "Roadnik" apart from other location-sharing apps is that it doesn't require registration or collect any personal data. And don't worry about ads - there are none! All location data is tied to a unique key that you can create yourself. So you can stay connected with the people who matter most to you without any unnecessary hassle.

App is designed to be simple and easy to use. Experience the peace of mind that comes with knowing your loved ones can always find you when you need it.

Features:
  - Share your location without registration and ads.
  - Rooms - up to 100 users can share the same map. Ideal for meetings in new places!
  - Notifications - get informed about new tracks.
  - Discord Rich Presence - automatically show your approximate location, speed, and heart rate as a Discord activity while sharing, with a direct link to your live map.
  - Battery friendly (well, geolocation is always power-consuming, but app do it's best to prevent fast draining).
